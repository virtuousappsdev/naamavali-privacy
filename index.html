Privacy Policy

Naamavali — Hindu Baby Name App • Effective: February 27, 2026

Summary: Naamavali is an offline-first app. Your data stays on your device. We do not require any account, login, or personal identification. We use Google AdMob for ads, and an optional AI feature that sends only your naming preferences (never your identity) to generate name suggestions.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide (Optional)

All of the following is optional and entered at your discretion during onboarding or in Settings:

  • Baby's gender — Boy, Girl, or Not decided
  • Region preference — Your cultural region within India or Indian Diaspora
  • Baby's birth date — Used solely to calculate Nakshatra (Vedic birth star)
  • Baby's birth time — Used solely for precise Nakshatra/Pada calculation
  • Baby's birth city — Used solely for timezone-accurate Nakshatra calculation

This information is stored only on your device using Android's DataStore and is never uploaded to our servers.

1.2 Information Generated by Your Usage

  • Favorite names — Names you save, stored locally on your device
  • Search history — Recent search queries, stored locally on your device
  • Personal notes — Notes you add to saved names, stored locally on your device

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your baby's chosen name
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Device identifiers (we do not read your Advertising ID; however, see Section 3 regarding AdMob)

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • Device model and OS version
  • IP address (used to infer general location at country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

We do not pass any of your baby's birth details, gender, or preferences to Google AdMob. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

3.2 AI Name Generation (Optional Feature)

If you choose to use the AI-powered name suggestion feature, the following preferences are sent to Anthropic's API to generate personalized name suggestions:

  • Selected gender, naming style, meaning theme, and preferred name length
  • Selected region and Nakshatra syllable (if birth details were provided)

The following is never sent to the AI service:

  • Your baby's birth date, time, or location
  • Your identity, device ID, or any personal contact information

This feature is entirely optional and clearly labeled. AI-generated names are stored locally on your device. Anthropic processes data under its own privacy policy: Anthropic Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All user data is stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted DataStore and SQLite databases.
  • All network communication (AdMob, AI API) uses HTTPS encryption.
  • We do not operate any servers that store your personal data.
  • Android's automatic backup may include your app preferences and favorites if you have device backup enabled. You can disable this in your Android system settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data exists only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • You can clear your preferences at any time from Settings → Reset All Preferences within the app.
  • You can remove individual favorites and search history from within the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Naamavali is a baby naming tool intended for use by parents and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require or request any information about the app user's identity — only optional details about a baby's birth for astrological calculations.
