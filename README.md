# Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — Naamavali

**Last updated:** March 2, 2026

Naamavali ("collection of names") is a Hindu baby naming app developed by Virtuous Apps. This document covers our privacy policy and terms of use.

---

## Privacy Policy

### 1. Information We Collect

**Birth details you provide (optional):**
- Baby's birth date, time, and city
- Gender preference

This information is stored **only on your device** using local storage. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

**Saved favorites and search history:**
- Names you save to favorites and recent searches are stored locally on your device only.

### 2. AI Name Generation

When you use the Nakshatra-based name generation feature, we send the following to our AI service to generate name suggestions:
- The Nakshatra syllable(s) selected
- Gender preference (if set)
- Previously generated names (to avoid duplicates)

**We do not send** your birth date, time, city, or any personally identifiable information to the AI service. The AI service processes the request and returns name suggestions. Generated names are cached on your device for offline access.

### 3. Advertising

Naamavali uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This may include:
- Device identifiers (advertising ID)
- General location (based on IP address)
- App usage data for ad personalization

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings:
- **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

### 4. Data Storage

All user data (birth details, favorites, preferences, cached names) is stored **locally on your device** using encrypted local databases. We do not operate any servers that store your personal data.

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

### 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:
- **Google AdMob**: collects advertising-related data as described above
- **AI name generation service**: receives only syllable and gender data (no personal information) as described in Section 2

### 6. Children's Privacy

Naamavali is a baby naming tool intended for use by parents and families. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The birth details entered are about the baby being named, not the app user, and are stored only on the device.

### 7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data:
- All network requests use HTTPS encryption
- Local data is stored in the app's private storage, inaccessible to other apps
- No personal data is transmitted to or stored on external servers

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

### 2. App Purpose

Naamavali provides Hindu baby name suggestions based on Vedic astrology (Nakshatra/Rashi) and numerology (Chaldean/Pythagorean). The astrological and numerological calculations are provided for cultural and informational purposes. They should not be considered as professional astrological advice.

### 3. AI-Generated Content

Name suggestions, meanings, and descriptions are generated using artificial intelligence. While we strive for cultural accuracy, AI-generated content may occasionally contain inaccuracies. Users should verify important details independently.

### 4. Accuracy of Calculations

The Nakshatra calculator uses the Meeus astronomical algorithm with Lahiri Ayanamsa. While accurate to approximately 0.5 degrees (sufficient for Nakshatra and Pada determination), it is a simplified implementation and may differ slightly from professional Jyotish software.
